# Year 12–13 IB Diploma Biology (SL & HL) Study Guide

Interactive study guide, procedural drill engine, mock-exam suite, Bio Stats & Formula Vault, and Scientific Investigation (IA) toolkit for **IB Diploma Biology (First Assessment 2025)** — covering both DP1 and DP2 across all 4 Themes (`A1.1–D4.3`, all 40 official sub-topics) with a live **SL / HL toggle**.

## Highlights
- **8 Comprehensive IB Topics (`A1.1–D4.3`):** Full SL + HL syllabus across Molecules, Cells, Organisms, and Ecosystems with HL-only sub-topics (`A2.1`, `A2.3`, `A3.2`, `B3.3`, `C2.1`, `D2.2`) and HL extensions tagged `level: "HL"`.
- **FA2025 Assessment Architecture (NO Paper 3):** Mock Paper 1A (MCQ), Paper 1B (Data-Based & Experimental Skills), and Paper 2 (SL & HL structured + markband extended responses).
- **Interactive Biology Explorables:** Custom SVG simulators per topic (magnification & DNA base pairing, cladistics & Simpson's D, membrane transport & SA:V, Bohr shift & sarcomere contraction, enzyme inhibition & action potential oscilloscope, capture–mark–recapture & χ², codon/mutation sandbox & water potential Ψ, Punnett/dihybrid genetics & Hardy–Weinberg).
- **IA Scientific Investigation (`/ia`):** Complete 2025 4×6 criteria guide, IB Animal Experimentation & biosafety guardrails, statistical test guide, and interactive self-audit.
- **Zero-Login iPad Persistence:** All progress, Daily 5, SRS review, streaks, and SL/HL selection save automatically in `localStorage`.
